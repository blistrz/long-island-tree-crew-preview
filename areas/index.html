Questions

Service area questions

Which areas do you serve?

Every community in Nassau County: 121 towns, villages and hamlets, from our base in Franklin Square. Western Suffolk comes next.

Do you charge more for areas farther away?

Most of Nassau is within a short drive of Franklin Square. If a job is far enough to change the price, the written estimate says so.

Do you work outside these areas?

For larger jobs, often yes. Send a photo and the address and we will tell you.

Are the tree rules the same everywhere?

No. Each town, city and village sets its own. We check the rule for your address before we quote.

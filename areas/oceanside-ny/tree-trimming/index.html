Tree trimming in Oceanside: what changes the job

Bay wind puts heavy loads on dense crowns. Thinning with proper cuts lets wind pass through, and clearing limbs off roofs reduces storm damage.

Salt-stressed trees need gentle pruning: we remove dead wood and avoid taking too much live growth at once.

Rules for this job in Oceanside

Pruning your own tree does not usually need a Town permit. Curbside trees are the Town's.

Signs your tree needs trimming

  • Dead branches in the crown
  • Limbs touching the roof, chimney or gutters
  • Low limbs over the driveway or sidewalk
  • Branches rubbing against each other
  • A lopsided or very heavy crown

Tree trimming questions from Oceanside

Should I prune a salt-damaged tree?

Remove the dead wood only, and give it a season to show what is still alive.

When is the best time to thin before storms?

Late fall through winter, ahead of nor'easter and hurricane seasons.

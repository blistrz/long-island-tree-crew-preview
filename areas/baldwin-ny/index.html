Local know-how

What to know about trees in Baldwin

Baldwin has gone by several names over the years, including Hick's Neck, Milburn, Baldwinsville and Baldwins. Since 1990 the Census has counted the area south of Atlantic Avenue separately as Baldwin Harbor, though it still shares Baldwin's ZIP code, school district and library.

The Census puts Baldwin's median year built at 1949 and the median home value near $594,000. Many yard trees went in with late-1940s homes and are now seventy-five years old or more.

Closer to the bay, wet ground and wind off the water add storm risk. Inland, age is the bigger issue: old wounds, deadwood and limbs grown out over roofs.

How our permit help works

Baldwin is an unincorporated hamlet in the Town of Hempstead. The Town Code requires a permit from the Commissioner of Highways before anyone removes or substantially alters a tree in the Town curbside area.

Trees on private lots are generally removable without a Town permit, according to local reporting on the Town's rules, unless the work is part of a building project.

Freeport, next door, is an incorporated village with its own code and its own electric utility. If you live near the line, check which side you are on.

Tree service questions from Baldwin

Updated November 16, 2026.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Baldwin?

Curbside trees need a Town of Hempstead Highway permit. Trees on your lot generally do not, unless the work is tied to a building project.

Is Baldwin Harbor part of Baldwin?

It shares Baldwin's ZIP code, school district and library, but the Census has counted it separately since 1990. Both follow Town of Hempstead rules.

How old are Baldwin's trees?

With a median year built of 1949, many are seventy-five years old or more.

What does tree removal cost in Baldwin?

Most Nassau removals fall between $650 and $2,500.

How fast can you come after a storm?

We answer storm calls around the clock and handle trees on homes first.

My property is near Freeport. Whose rules apply?

Where the trunk stands decides it. Freeport is a village with its own code.

Do you grind stumps?

Yes, below grade, with the chips raked in or hauled.

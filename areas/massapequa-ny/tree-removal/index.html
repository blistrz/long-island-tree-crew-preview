Tree removal in Massapequa: what changes the job

Sandy brought flooding and downed trees to Massapequa in 2012, and wet ground near the water still weakens roots. Removals near canals are planned so nothing lands in the water or on a dock.

Before any cutting, the Town of Oyster Bay's Chapter 225 calls for a permit application to the Department of Parks, filed at least 10 workdays ahead.

Rules for this job in Massapequa

The fee is $5 per inch of combined trunk diameter, capped at $75 per tree, and can be waived or reduced for three or fewer trees. Trees confirmed dead, diseased or severely damaged, and emergencies, are exempt.

Signs a tree needs to come down

  • No leaves in summer, or bark falling off in sheets
  • A new lean, or soil heaving on one side of the base
  • Mushrooms or hollow sounds at the trunk
  • Large cracks where two main stems meet
  • Roots lifting the driveway or the foundation

Tree removal questions from Massapequa

Is a storm-damaged tree exempt from the permit?

If it threatens life or property, or the Parks Commissioner confirms it is severely damaged, yes.

Can you remove a tree beside my canal?

Yes. We keep debris out of the water and off the dock.

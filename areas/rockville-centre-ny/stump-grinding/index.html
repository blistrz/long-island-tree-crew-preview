Stump grinding in Rockville Centre: what changes the job

We grind stumps below the lawn line and remove nearby surface roots, so the spot is ready for a new tree or lawn.

Rockville Centre's code bars poplars, willows and swamp maples on village streets and within 25 feet of water conduits, sewer mains and storm drains. We help you pick a replacement that avoids them.

Rules for this job in Rockville Centre

Grinding a stump on your property needs no separate permit, but your replacement tree should follow the village's planting limits.

Why people remove stumps

  • Mowing around it every week
  • Planning a patio, fence or pool
  • New sprouts keep coming up from the roots
  • It is a trip hazard near a walkway
  • Insects or fungus have moved in

The county at a glance

An old canopy over postwar homes

Nassau's 121 communities hold about 373,000 owner-occupied homes. In 92 of them, half the homes were built before 1960, and many of the street and yard trees went in at the same time.

Three towns and two cities split the county, and 57 of the communities we serve are incorporated villages with their own codes on top. That patchwork is why we check the permit rule for every address.

Data: U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2020–2024 5-year estimates.

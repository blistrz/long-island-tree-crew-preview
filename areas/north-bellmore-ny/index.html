Tree rules in North Bellmore

North Bellmore is an unincorporated hamlet, so the Town of Hempstead code applies. Curbside trees need a permit from the Commissioner of Highways; local reporting describes yard trees as removable without a Town permit.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in North Bellmore?

For a curbside tree, yes: the Town of Hempstead requires a Highway permit. For trees on your own lot, generally not, unless the removal is part of a building project.

What does tree removal cost in North Bellmore?

Most Nassau removals fall between $650 and $2,500, depending on size, access and how close the tree is to the house.

About the crew

A Franklin Square tree crew for Nassau County

Long Island Tree Crew removes, trims and cleans up trees for homeowners, HOAs and businesses across Nassau County. We are based in Franklin Square, in the Town of Hempstead.

Who we are

Who you deal with

You deal with the people who do the work. The person who quotes your job is on site when it is done, and the number on this website reaches the crew.

Most of our work is the tree near the house: the big oak over the roof, the dead maple by the garage, the limb that came down in the last storm. We take those apart carefully, and we leave the yard clean.

Safety

How we keep jobs safe

Tree work is dangerous. These are the habits we keep on every job.

Tied in, always

Climbers work on ropes and saddles, and pieces near structures are lowered on rigging lines.

Full protective gear

Helmets, eye and ear protection, chainsaw chaps and gloves, following ANSI Z133 practices for tree work.

Drop zone control

The work area is roped off, and nobody stands under the tree while pieces come down.

Lines are for the utility

We do not touch energized lines. PSEG Long Island makes them safe first.

Price in writing

Scope, price and permits on paper before any work starts.

Walk-through at the end

We walk the yard with you before we leave.

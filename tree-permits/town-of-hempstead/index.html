Tree permit guide

Tree removal permits in the Town of Hempstead

The Town of Hempstead covers much of southwest Nassau, including Franklin Square, Levittown, Oceanside and Merrick. Its tree rules focus on the curbside area, while incorporated villages inside the Town write their own.

Updated November 16, 2026. Rules change, so confirm with the Town of Hempstead before work starts.

Curbside trees need a Highway permit

The Town Code says no person may destroy, remove or substantially alter a tree in the Town curbside area without a permit from the Commissioner of Highways. That applies to homeowners and to utility companies alike.

The curbside area is the strip between the sidewalk and the street. Even when you mow it and rake it, a tree there is regulated by the Town.

Trees on your own property

In the unincorporated hamlets of the Town (Franklin Square, Levittown, Oceanside, East Meadow, Merrick and others), local reporting describes trees on private property as removable without a Town permit, while curbside trees need permission. Confirm with the Town before work starts, especially if the removal is part of a building project.

Villages inside the Town have their own rules

Incorporated villages such as Garden City, Rockville Centre, Valley Stream, Floral Park and Freeport have their own codes. Garden City, for example, requires a permit for every removal, and Valley Stream licenses tree removal businesses. Check your village before you check the Town.

Dead or dangerous curbside trees

If a curbside tree in front of your house is dead or failing, contact the Town. It can inspect the tree and arrange removal on request. Current fees and the request process are set by the Town, so confirm them directly.

Questions

Can I remove the tree between my sidewalk and the street?

Not without a permit from the Town's Commissioner of Highways. That strip is the Town curbside area.

Do I need a Town permit to remove a tree in my back yard?

In the unincorporated hamlets, usually not, unless the work is tied to a building permit. Villages inside the Town have their own rules.

Who handles a dead curbside tree?

The Town. Contact it to request an inspection and removal.

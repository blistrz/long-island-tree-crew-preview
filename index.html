Pricing

What tree work costs on Long Island

Public cost data puts the average Nassau County tree removal at about $973, and most jobs land between $650 and $2,500.

A tall oak hanging over a roof costs more than a maple in an open back yard, because it has to come down in small pieces on ropes. Wires, fences, pools and a long carry to the street add time. Stump grinding is priced on its own.

Tree health watch

Three threats to Long Island trees right now

These are the problems homeowners ask about most. If you see the signs, send us a photo.

Beech leaf disease

Dark bands between the leaf veins, then thinning crowns. Common on North Shore beeches.

Spotted lanternfly

Reports surged across Nassau and Suffolk in 2025. Tree of heaven is its favorite host.

Oak wilt

Found in Suffolk since 2016. Prune oaks in winter, when the beetles that spread it are inactive.

Questions

Tree service questions we hear every week

Can't find yours? Text it to us with a photo of the tree.

How much does tree removal cost on Long Island?

Public cost data puts the average Nassau County removal at about $973, with most jobs between $650 and $2,500. Height, trunk size, how close the tree is to the house or wires, and whether a crane is needed all move the price. See our price guide, or text a photo for a written price.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree?

It depends on where you live. The Town of Hempstead requires a Highway permit for trees in the curbside area, North Hempstead regulates many front-yard removals under Chapter 20A, and some villages, Garden City among them, require a permit for every removal. We check the rule for your address before we quote.

Who takes care of the tree between the sidewalk and the curb?

Usually your town or village. In the Town of Hempstead you need a permit from the Commissioner of Highways before anyone removes or cuts back a curbside tree. We tell you who to call and handle what we are allowed to.

Do you remove trees that touch power lines?

Branches on the main lines are PSEG Long Island's job, and nobody should touch them but the utility. We clear what is safe to clear, and we coordinate with PSEG when a job needs the line made safe first.

Do you grind stumps?

Yes. Add stump grinding to any removal, or book it on its own for old stumps. We grind below the lawn line so you can reseed or replant.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold a Nassau County Home Improvement License with the tree removal category and carry liability insurance. We send a certificate of insurance before the job if you ask.

How fast can you come after a storm?

We answer storm calls around the clock and go first to trees on houses, cars and driveways. Send a photo while you wait so we arrive with the right gear.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Nassau County, starting from our base in Franklin Square. Western Suffolk is next. See every area we serve.
